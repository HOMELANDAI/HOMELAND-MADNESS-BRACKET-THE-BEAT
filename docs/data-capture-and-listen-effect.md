# Data Capture and The Listen Effect

## Core Idea

HomelandAI Madness is not only a tournament. It is a cultural behavior engine. Every vote, preview play, bracket submission, replay, upset pick, and Legend alignment creates data that can become content.

The central data concept is:

## The Listen Effect

The Listen Effect measures how voter behavior changes after users hear preview clips before casting a vote.

In plain language:

> What did people think they wanted before listening, and what did they actually choose after hearing both beats?

## Why It Matters

Music debates are usually emotional and memory-driven. The Listen Effect helps separate:

- Nostalgia from fresh listening
- Artist-name bias from beat strength
- Instant voters from careful voters
- Committee expectations from actual fan response
- Legend opinions from mass cultural consensus

## Key Metrics

### Preview Play Rate
How many users played each preview before voting.

### Completion Rate
How many users listened to the full 10-30 seconds.

### Replay Rate
How often users replayed a preview.

### Skip Rate
Which previews were abandoned quickly.

### Vote Flip Rate
How many users changed expected picks after listening.

### Time-to-Vote
How long users took to vote after previews.

### Informed Vote Share
Vote percentage among users who listened to both previews.

### Instant Vote Share
Vote percentage among users who voted without listening.

### Underdog Lift
How much support a lower seed gained after preview exposure.

### Name Bias Gap
Difference between visible-name voting and Blind Mode voting.

### Legend Alignment Rate
How often fans agreed with the Legend guest.

### Committee Trust Score
How often committee seeds performed as expected after users listened.

## Fan Archetypes

The data can create user identities and badges:

- The Loyalist: votes quickly for known favorites
- The Crate Digger: listens to everything and backs underdogs
- The Strategist: predicts what the masses will choose
- The Rebel: often goes against fan consensus or Legend picks
- The Purist: votes based on technical production values
- The Crowd Reader: performs well in prediction brackets
- The Upset Hunter: specializes in lower-seed wins

## Example Insight

> Before previews, 68% of prediction brackets had the #4 seed advancing. But after users listened to both previews, the #12 seed captured 53% of informed votes. That suggests the lower seed was undervalued by memory and gained support through fresh listening.
