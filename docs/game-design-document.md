# Game Design Document

## Title

**HomelandAI Madness: Bracket the Beat**

## Brand

By HAI / HomelandAI

## Core Premise

HomelandAI Madness is a competitive voting-style music tournament where beats or songs are seeded into a bracket and fans decide which entries advance. It can be played live in person, remotely through the HomelandAI app, on Twitch as a hype broadcast, or on Maestro.tv as a premium interactive experience.

## Core Design Thesis

Music fans already argue about rankings, impact, producers, eras, and regions. HomelandAI Madness turns that natural behavior into a structured competitive experience with prediction brackets, committees, prize pools, live voting, data analytics, and aftershow content.

## Primary Player Types

### Fan Voter
Votes matchup by matchup, reacts to results, earns badges, and participates in the culture debate.

### Prediction Player
Fills out a full bracket before voting starts and tries to predict what the masses will choose.

### Committee Member
Pays a mandatory buy-in, helps select and seed a region, and earns points based on how well the region performs.

### Premium Member
Gets access to aftershows, data breakdowns, Legend Mode reveals, Blind Mode results, and insider discussions.

### Legend Guest
A producer, artist, DJ, or cultural figure who locks in a live vote and gives commentary on how they think the culture will vote.

## Core Mechanics

### Bracket Format

Supported sizes:

- 16 entries
- 32 entries
- 64 entries

Suggested regions:

- East Coast
- West Coast
- South
- Midwest / International

### Selection Sunday

Selection Sunday is the reveal event where committees announce seeds and defend their choices. The seeding itself should create conversation, disagreement, harmony, strife, passion, and debate.

### Fan Prediction Brackets

Fans fill out brackets before the tournament starts. They are not simply voting for personal taste. They are predicting what the culture will choose.

Scoring:

- Round of 32: 1 point
- Sweet 16: 2 points
- Elite 8: 4 points
- Final 4: 8 points
- Championship: 16 points

Tie-breaker:

- Predict total championship-round votes
- Loyalty or leaderboard status can break further ties

### Phone Voting

Live and remote voting should happen through the HomelandAI app or web app. Phone voting minimizes peer pressure in live rooms and creates owned first-party data.

Principles:

- One phone, one vote
- Private ballot mode
- Results hidden until voting closes
- Vote lock confirmation
- Anti-duplicate-vote checks

### Mini-Player Preview System

Each entry can have a 10-30 second preview player beside it. This helps remote fans compare beats before voting.

The production version should use licensed audio, cleared previews, official embeds, or approved platform integrations.

### Listen-to-Vote Bonus

Users earn XP or badges for listening to both previews before voting.

Badges:

- Fair Voter
- Crate Listener
- Heard Both Sides
- No Skip Selector
- Deep Crate Certified
- Culture Scout

### Blind Preview Mode

Artist name, title, album art, and seed number are hidden. Users vote based only on the sound.

Modes:

- Blind Beat Battle
- No Names Round
- Crate Test Mode
- Producer Ear Challenge
- Bias Breaker Round

### Legend Mode

A guest producer, artist, DJ, or cultural figure listens, locks in a vote, and the system reveals how that vote compares with fan consensus.

Possible outcomes:

- Legend Agreed With the Culture
- Fans Overruled the Legend
- Legend Called the Upset
- Rebel Pick Won
- Culture Split

### Committee Scoring

Committees earn points from:

- Song wins
- Upset bonuses
- Accuracy bonuses
- Fan grade multiplier
- Sleeper accuracy
- Seed respect
- Underdog discovery

### The Listen Effect

The Listen Effect measures how voting changes after fans hear the previews.

Example:

> Before previews, 71% of prediction brackets had the #2 seed winning. After both previews were played, the #11 seed captured 54% of live votes.

## Content Extensions

- The Listen Effect aftershow
- Committee on Trial
- Fan Consensus vs Legend Vote
- Name Power vs Beat Power
- Blind Mode Shock
- Upset Watch
- Staff Roundtable
- Data Desk

## Win Conditions

### For Beat/Song Entries
Advance through bracket and become champion.

### For Fans
Score highest in prediction bracket, call upsets, build loyalty status, and earn badges.

### For Committees
Seed the strongest and most culturally accurate region.

### For HomelandAI
Capture engagement, produce recurring content, build data IP, and create a premium cultural franchise.
