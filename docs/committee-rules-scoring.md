# Committee Rules and Scoring Guide

## Purpose

Committees make HomelandAI Madness more strategic. They select and seed regions, defend their choices on Selection Sunday, and compete for reputation and payout based on how well their seeds perform.

## Mandatory Buy-In

Committee buy-in is mandatory at **$49.99 per committee member**.

This creates:

- Skin in the game
- Seriousness
- Prize pool funding
- Status and exclusivity
- Accountability for seed decisions

## Regional Committee Structure

Suggested regions:

- East Coast
- West Coast
- South
- Midwest / International

Each committee is responsible for:

- Selecting beats/songs for its region
- Assigning seeds
- Verifying title, artist, producer, and release year
- Defending controversial picks
- Appearing in Selection Sunday or aftershow segments

## Committee Scoring

### Base Points

- Round of 32 win: 10 points
- Sweet 16 win: 15 points
- Elite 8 win: 20 points
- Final 4 win: 25 points
- Championship win: 50 points

### Upset Bonuses

- 5-8 seed defeats 1-4 seed: +10 points
- 9-12 seed defeats 1-8 seed: +20 points
- 13-16 seed defeats any seed: +30 points

### Accuracy Bonuses

- #1 or #2 seed reaches Final Four: +15 points
- #3 seed or lower reaches Final Four: +25 points

### Fan Grade Multiplier

Fans rate committee seeding in the HomelandAI app.

- 4.5-5.0 stars: x1.2
- 3.5-4.4 stars: x1.1
- Below 3.5: no multiplier

## Listen Effect Committee Additions

### Sleeper Accuracy Bonus
Committee gets bonus points if a lower seed they placed advances after high preview engagement.

### Seed Respect Score
Measures whether fans agreed with seed placement after listening.

### Overseed Penalty
A high seed loses credibility if it performs poorly among users who listened to both previews.

### Underdog Discovery Bonus
Rewards committees that introduce overlooked beats that gain support after preview exposure.

## Prize Split Example

If 4 committees with 5 members each pay $49.99, the pool is about $1,000.

Suggested monthly distribution:

- 1st place committee: 50%
- 2nd place committee: 25%
- 3rd place committee: 15%
- 4th place committee: 10%

## Committee Content Role

Committees should create drama and credibility. They can appear in:

- Selection Sunday
- Committee Hot Seat
- Committee on Trial
- Aftershow debates
- Seed controversy segments
- Data Lab reviews

## Committee Questions

- What did you see that fans missed?
- Did you seed for legacy, beat quality, popularity, or matchup drama?
- Which result embarrassed the committee?
- Which pick do you feel vindicated by?
- Did the fan vote prove your region was balanced?
- Did Blind Mode expose an overseed?
