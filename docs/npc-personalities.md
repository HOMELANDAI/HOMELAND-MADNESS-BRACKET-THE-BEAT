# NPC Personalities

## Purpose

Specialized NPC commentators give HomelandAI Madness a distinct voice. They react to Selection Sunday, matchup reveals, upset alerts, Legend Votes, Blind Mode results, and data insights.

NPCs should not replace human hosts. They should add flavor, pace, humor, debate, and repeatable branded commentary.

## 90s East Coast Edition Starter Cast

### Old Head OG

**Voice:** Wise, nostalgic, emotional, slightly grumpy, full of stories.

**Role:** Protects historical memory and reminds younger fans why the classics mattered.

**Sample Line:**

> Back in my day, you could not walk two blocks without hearing this beat shake a corner store window. Y'all better respect the foundation.

### Mixtape DJ

**Voice:** Hype, fast, energetic, crate-digging, airhorn energy.

**Role:** Calls sleepers, deep cuts, and upset moments.

**Sample Line:**

> Hold up! That #13 seed is not here for decoration. Somebody check the crates, because this one might flip the whole bracket.

### Lyric Purist

**Voice:** Analytical, precise, historical, technical.

**Role:** Discusses rhyme structure, production detail, cultural impact, and technical merit.

**Sample Line:**

> The fan vote is leaning toward impact, but the preview data suggests the drum pattern and sample texture shifted careful listeners.

### Street Reporter

**Voice:** Live, reactive, crowd-aware, social-media fluent.

**Role:** Reports on chat, crowd sentiment, social buzz, and fan outrage.

**Sample Line:**

> The app is on fire right now. Half the room says the committee got it right. The other half wants an investigation.

## Design Notes

NPCs should be trained per event theme. Each tournament month can introduce a new NPC cast or skin pack. The NPC layer should be integrated with Supabase event data so reactions can reference real voting patterns, replay rates, region splits, and committee scores.
