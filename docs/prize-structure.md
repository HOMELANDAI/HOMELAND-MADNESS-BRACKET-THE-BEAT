# Prize Structure

## Source Model

The HomelandAI prize model is based on a recurring contest structure with 12 monthly contests, tiered buy-ins, cash winners, consolation giveaways, timed free access to media, leaderboard-based eligibility, sponsored special contests, yearly retreats, and loyalty-based tie-breakers.

## Monthly Contest Model

- 12 monthly contests
- Tiered fan buy-ins: $19.99, $29.99, $49.99
- Committee buy-in: $49.99 mandatory
- Monthly prize distribution
- Cash winners
- Consolation giveaways
- Timed media access
- Leaderboard status determines grand prize eligibility
- February and December can have special sponsored contests with more winners
- Yearly Homeland retreats
- Early bird legacy member option: $99.99
- Possible creative bonuses for legacy members

## Grand Prize Eligibility

- Level 1: Must enter 10 of 12 contests
- Level 2: Must enter 7 of 12 contests
- Level 3: Must enter 4 of 12 contests

Tie-breakers can be broken by loyalty rewards or leaderboard status.

## Fan Prediction Prize Logic

Fans compete by predicting the bracket outcome.

Scoring:

- Round of 32: 1 point
- Sweet 16: 2 points
- Elite 8: 4 points
- Final 4: 8 points
- Championship: 16 points

Tie-breaker:

- Closest prediction for total championship-round vote count
- Secondary tie-breaker: loyalty status
- Third tie-breaker: leaderboard history

## Committee Prize Logic

Committee members pay a mandatory $49.99 buy-in and compete by building the strongest region.

Suggested split:

- 1st place committee: 50%
- 2nd place committee: 25%
- 3rd place committee: 15%
- 4th place committee: 10%

## Premium Content Value

Higher tiers can include:

- Aftershow access
- Data dashboard access
- Legend Mode recaps
- Blind Mode reveal sessions
- Committee room discussions
- NFT or badge claims
- Early access to next bracket speculation
- Downloadable Culture Reports

## Compliance Note

Paid contests, cash prizes, loyalty rewards, sponsor placements, and music usage should be reviewed for contest law, tax reporting, regional eligibility, and music licensing before public commercial launch.
